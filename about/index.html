People who Genuinely Care.

This is where you can learn who we are and decide whether our values, passion, and commitment align with your needs. We believe that great results come from working with people who genuinely care about what they do. If we were the client, we would want to work with a team that is passionate about the service we need.

Our production teams are made up of people who genuinely understand the craft behind what they do. For our video production projects, our filmmakers have contributed to professional movie productions. For our DJ services, our DJs are experienced performers with advanced mixing skills, including live mash-ups, scratching, and professional EDM festival-level performance.

We proudly serve local businesses, corporations, organizations, and artists. Whether you are launching a brand, promoting an organization, producing an event, or creating content for your audience, our goal is to bring your vision to life through creative, high quality solutions tailored to your needs.

We are committed to delivering exceptional quality, professional service, and outstanding value in every project we undertake. But our work goes beyond serving our clients. We are passionate about supporting local businesses and organizations, helping them grow, strengthen their communities, and create new opportunities within the cities we serve.

At the heart of RECROC, it is simple: we want to do great work with people who care about what they do, help our clients achieve what they are trying to achieve, and create opportunities for the people and communities around us.

Why us

Why RECROC?

As a hybrid production company and creative agency, RECROC gives you direct access to the people who are actually involved in the creative work. You work with people who understand the cameras, computers, scripts, music, and creative process behind your project.

Our team is led by filmmakers, DJs, composers, and other creative professionals who understand their craft firsthand. We don't believe creative work should be one-size fits all. Your project should have its own identity, built around your goals, your audience, and what makes your organization different. That's why we approach every project with fresh ideas, strategic thinking, and a genuine commitment to the work.

We believe every project deserves fresh thinking. Whether we're building a brand, producing a commercial, creating an event experience, or developing something entirely new, we approach the work with creativity, strategic thinking, and attention to detail.

Most importantly, we want RECROC to be easy to work with. We take the time to understand what you need, communicate clearly throughout the process, deliver on time, and take responsibility for the quality of the work we produce. Our goal isn't simply to complete your project. It's to create work that strengthens your image, builds trust, and creates lasting value for your organization.

Vision

Where we are going.

RECROC vision is to become a leading media company that makes professional media, creative talent, and opportunities accessible to businesses, organizations, and creators across the United States. We envision RECROC becoming a trusted destination where businesses can build powerful brands, organizations can bring their ideas to life, and creators can find the resources, people, and opportunities they need to pursue their ambitions. At the same time, we aim to build a company where passionate creatives can develop their skills, build meaningful careers, and grow without being limited by traditional industry gatekeeping. Ultimately, we want RECROC to create lasting value not only for our clients, but also for the people who build the company and the creative work behind it.

Mission

RecRoc exists to help businesses and organizations build media identities that strengthen their reputation, earn trust, and drive profitable growth.

Values4

What we hold to.

Real Creativity With Soul

We don't settle for soulless work when we have the ability to create something meaningful.

People First

We believe great work starts with great people. We build relationships based on respect, trust, communication, and genuine care for the people we work with. We want our clients to feel supported, our creators to feel valued, and our employees to have an environment where they can do their best work and grow.

Shared Success

We believe our success is connected to the success of the people we work with. When our clients grow and achieve their goals, RECROC grows with them. We also want the people who build RECROC to grow alongside the company, giving our employees and creators opportunities to develop their skills, take on greater responsibilities, and build their careers.

Open Opportunity

We believe talent should have the opportunity to learn, grow, and contribute. We don't believe knowledge should be unnecessarily hidden or opportunities reserved only for people who already have connections or experience. We encourage our people to ask questions, learn from one another, explore new roles, and build the skills they need to pursue the careers they want. Opportunity still requires effort and talent, but everyone deserves a chance to find their path.
