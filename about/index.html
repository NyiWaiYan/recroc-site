About / RecRoc

Peoplewhogenuinelycare.

Decide for yourself whether our values line up with what you need. If we were the client, we would want a team that cares about the exact service we are buying.

Start a project How we work

Who we are

Our production teams are made of people who understand the craft behind the work. The filmmakers have contributed to professional movie productions. The DJs are performing artists with advanced mixing skills, live mash-ups, scratching, and EDM festival level sets behind them.

We work with local businesses, corporations, organisations and artists. Launching a brand, promoting an organisation, producing an event, making something for an audience: the job is to get your idea into a finished piece without the usual friction.

The work goes past the client list. We want local businesses and organisations to grow, because that strengthens the communities and creates opportunities in the cities we work in.

Mission

RecRoc exists to help businesses and organisations build media identities that strengthen their reputation, earn trust, and drive profitable growth.

Why RecRoc / 03

Directaccesstothemakers.

A hybrid production company and creative agency, which in practice means fewer people between the brief and the camera.

01

Direct access to the makers

Instead of putting account managers between you and the production team, you work with the people who understand the cameras, the computers, the scripts, the music. Led by filmmakers, DJs and composers who know their craft firsthand.

02

Nothing one size fits all

Your project gets its own identity, built around your goals, your audience, and what makes your organisation different. Fresh thinking on every brief, whether it is a brand, a commercial, an event or something we have not built before.

03

Easy to work with

We take the time to understand what you need, communicate clearly through the process, deliver on time, and take responsibility for the quality of what we produce. The goal is not to complete your project. It is to make something that holds up.

Values / 04

What we hold to, including when it costs us.

01   Real creativity with soulWe do not settle for soulless work when we have the ability to make something meaningful.
02   People firstGreat work starts with great people. Relationships built on respect, trust, communication and genuine care. Clients supported, creators valued, employees given room to do their best work.
03   Shared successOur success is tied to the success of the people we work with. When clients grow, RecRoc grows. The people who build RecRoc grow with it.
04   Open opportunityKnowledge should not be hidden and opportunity should not be reserved for people who already have the connections. Ask questions, learn from each other, take on new roles. Effort and talent still decide, but everyone deserves the chance to find their path.
