Services / DJ and Live Events

School&CampusEvents

DJing is a live performance, not pressing play.

Start a project

Campus events build school spirit and stay in people's memory for years, which is a lot to ask of a laptop on shuffle.

We work with colleges, universities, high schools, middle schools, student organisations, campus activity boards and educational institutions. Homecoming, prom, winter formal, welcome week, orientation, cultural celebrations, graduation parties, pep rallies and student festivals.

Our DJs perform live on professional industry controllers. Every transition, selection and energy shift is mixed in real time and adapted to the crowd in front of them. That is the difference between a set and a playlist, and students notice it immediately.

We plan with school administrators, student leaders and activity coordinators so the event is safe, organised and properly run from the first song to the last.
