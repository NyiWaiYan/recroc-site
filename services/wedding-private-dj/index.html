Services / DJ and Live Events

Wedding&PrivateCelebrations

The biggest celebrations deserve more than background music.

Start a project

Some celebrations only happen once, and they should sound the way you imagined them.

We DJ weddings, engagement parties, rehearsal dinners, bachelor and bachelorette parties, anniversaries, birthdays, yacht parties and private milestones. Thirty guests or three hundred, the set is built around your personality and the atmosphere you want people to walk into.

Every song, every transition and every moment that matters should happen on your terms rather than a generic running order. We go through your musical preferences, the traditions that matter, the must plays and the do not plays, before the night.

Our DJs mix live on professional industry controllers, not pre made playlists. An elegant first dance, an emotional father daughter dance and a packed EDM floor at midnight are three different jobs, and they get read in real time.
