Hiring well starts with a first impression you control.

Candidates want more than a job description. They want to see the culture, the values and the people. A recruitment video gives them an honest look at the workplace before they ever submit an application, which means the applications you get are better matched.

One role or a full campaign, the video does the same job: it builds credibility with the people you want and quietly filters out the people you do not.
