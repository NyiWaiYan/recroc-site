BeatProductionforSingers&Songwriters

Every great song begins with the right production.

Start a project

Every artist has a sound. The production underneath it should not be shared with strangers.

We produce custom beats for singers, songwriters, independent artists, record labels and music creators. First demo or next release, every production is built from the ground up around your vision, not assembled from recycled loops or pre made templates.

Our producers work through your musical style, influences, references and goals before writing anything. Melody, chord progression, drum pattern, arrangement: each decision is made to support your vocal and your songwriting.

Unlike leased beats that several artists can buy, these are made for your project only. Pop ballad, hip hop record, R&B track, EDM anthem, cinematic composition. The goal is a foundation you are proud to release.
