Overview

Documentary is not interviews plus footage. It is deciding what a story is actually about, then earning the right to tell it.

Nonprofit missions, community initiatives, company culture, a personal journey. We plan carefully, film patiently, and build the structure before we start rather than hoping it appears in the edit.

The result is something that informs, moves people, and holds up years later.
