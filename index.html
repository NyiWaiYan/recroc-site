About

Small studio. Big craft.

RecRoc is a hybrid production company and creative agency based in North Dakota. Our filmmakers have contributed to professional movie productions, and our DJs perform with festival-level mixing backgrounds. We’re committed to helping local businesses and entrepreneurs grow because when businesses thrive in our communities, it creates more opportunities for everyone.

Process

Four steps. Zero chaos.

  1. The call

    Discover

    A 30-minute call. Your goals, audience, and budget, with no jargon and no pitch deck.

  2. The blueprint

    Plan

    Script, shot list, schedule, and a fixed quote. You approve everything before we roll.

  3. The shoot

    Produce

    Shoot day or show day. Small crew, professional kit, and a set that runs on time.

  4. The handoff

    Deliver

    Edit, color, sound. Final files cut for every platform you publish on.
