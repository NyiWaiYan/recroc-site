About

People who genuinely care.

Great results come from working with people who genuinely care about what they do. If we were the client, we’d want a team that’s passionate about the exact service we need — so that’s the team we built.

Our production team are filmmakers who have contributed to professional movie productions. Our DJs are performers with advanced mixing skills — live mash-ups, scratching, and EDM festival-level performance backgrounds. We proudly serve local businesses, corporations, organizations, and artists across North Dakota and the Midwest.

Whether you’re launching a brand, promoting an organization, producing an event, or creating content for your audience, our goal is to bring your vision to life through creative, high-quality work tailored to your needs.

Our mission

Helping local businesses and entrepreneurs launch and grow, while making projects easier for corporations across North Dakota and the Midwest.

When businesses grow in our communities, it creates more opportunities for our people and our students.

Our process

Simple. Start to finish.

From the first conversation to final delivery, every step is organized, efficient, and built to make your experience as smooth as possible.

  1. Step 01

    Listen

    Every project begins with understanding your goals, ideas, and expectations, so we build the right creative direction together.

  2. Step 02

    Strategize

    Once we understand your vision, we create a clear plan before production begins, keeping everyone aligned and the project moving.

  3. Step 03

    Create

    Our team handles production with attention to detail, making sure every shot, sound, and design serves a purpose.

  4. Step 04

    Refine

    Before final delivery, you review the project and request revisions. Delivering exactly what you want is our top priority.

  5. Step 05

    Deliver

    Once approved, we deliver in high-quality formats ready for websites, social media, advertising, print, or wherever you need it.
